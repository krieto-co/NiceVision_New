Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our lenses, frames, and in-store policies.

Walk-ins are always welcome! You can stop by anytime during our store hours (Mon - Sat: 9:30 AM - 6:00 PM) to explore our frames, lenses, and available eyewear packages.

When you purchase select prescription lens packages, you can choose a frame from our designated collection for free. In addition, any total purchase of $200 USD or more automatically receives an instant 10% discount!

If you have frames you already love, bring them to our Carrollton store. Our team can help you explore available prescription lens options for your existing frames.

We offer a full spectrum of lens solutions, from Single Vision ($70+) and Classic Bifocals ($85+) to Premium No-Line Progressives ($105+) and durable Kids Eyewear ($65+). You can customize your lenses with anti-reflective glare protection, blue-light filtering, UV-blocking transition (photochromic) technology, and scratch-resistant finishes.

Most prescription glasses are typically ready within 2 to 4 business days. Some lens options may require additional processing time. We will contact you when your order is ready for pickup.

"Frames That Fit Your World." With a trusted heritage of eyewear experience, we provide the DFW community with high-grade prescription optical lenses, designer frames, and approachable, caring service.
